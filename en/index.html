No. Método REI treats wealth more deeply: self-government, identity, financial intelligence, purpose and legacy.

No. It's for anyone ready to grow, mature, govern their own life better and prosper with purpose.

Yes. Método REI is the gateway to a deeper journey of transformation and whole-life government.
