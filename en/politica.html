Refund policy & terms

Full refund up to 7 days before the event. Request it on WhatsApp or by replying to your confirmation email with your order number. The money goes back to the same card within 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Less than 7 days before the event there are no refunds, but you can transfer your ticket to another person or to another Método REI edition at no cost, as long as you tell us at least 24 hours before it starts.

No-shows are not refunded or credited.

If the organizers cancel or postpone the event, you choose between using your ticket on the new date or getting a full refund.

Bonus materials (e-books and platform access) are delivered after purchase and stay yours even if you transfer the ticket.

Seating is first come, first served within your ticket's section. The organizers may use event photos and videos, in which attendees may appear, for promotion.

Payments are processed securely by Square. We never store your card details.
